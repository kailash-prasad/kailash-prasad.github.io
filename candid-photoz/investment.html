Before you book

A little more clarity.

Are these the final booking prices?

These prices and highlighted deliverables are transcribed from the packages displayed on the original website on 20 November 2026. Request a current written quotation; availability, taxes, travel, working hours and final inclusions must be confirmed by the studio.

Can we customise the coverage?

Use the enquiry form to describe your events and ask the studio for a tailored proposal. The team will need your dates, location and photography or film requirements.

How soon will our photographs and films arrive?

A delivery timeline is not confirmed in this preview. Ask for separate written milestones for the first preview, edited photographs, teaser, main film and albums before booking.

Who will photograph our wedding?

Ask which lead photographer and cinematographer will attend, how many people will cover each event, and what backup arrangements apply. The preview does not promise a particular team.

What does “raw photos” mean?

The original package artwork mentions raw photos in JPEG. Ask whether this means unedited JPEG images or camera RAW files, and confirm the number and format of files included.

Can we see a complete wedding?

Ask the studio to share a complete gallery and film from a celebration similar in scale and lighting to yours, in addition to the portfolio highlights.

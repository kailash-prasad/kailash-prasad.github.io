A little clarity

Before you decide.

Do we have to do a pre-wedding shoot?

It is optional. It can be a chance to spend time together, become familiar with the camera and create photographs for yourselves or your invitations. You can also focus your coverage on the wedding celebrations.

For your proposal: Ask how adding or removing a session changes the proposed coverage.

Discuss this question
Can we enquire about pre-wedding photographs without a film?

Yes. The enquiry form lets you request photographs only, films only, or both. Selecting an option starts a discussion; it does not reserve a team or confirm a package.

For your proposal: The studio needs to confirm the available service combination and quotation.

Discuss this question
How long should our pre-wedding session be?

Build the session around usable photography time. Travel, outfit changes, hair and makeup, entry checks and breaks all use part of the day. The schedule worksheet helps you see the trade-offs before choosing too many stops.

For your proposal: The final duration and coverage hours belong in your proposal.

Discuss this question
How many outfits should we bring?

Choose a manageable number that you can move, sit and walk in comfortably. Each change also needs somewhere private and time for adjustments. Use the schedule worksheet to check whether your changes fit the session.

For your proposal: Confirm included outfit changes and changing arrangements for your chosen location.

Discuss this question
Are makeup, hair, clothes and props included?

These are separate planning items unless your proposal explicitly includes them. Tell the studio whether you want help arranging them, then confirm who books and pays each supplier.

For your proposal: This preview does not promise rental outfits, a makeup artist or props.

Discuss this question
Does an entry ticket allow a professional photoshoot?

Do not assume so. A venue may have a separate professional photo/video booking, crew limit or charge. Our location guides link to official information where available.

For your proposal: Agree who arranges permission and obtain current venue approval before travelling.

Discuss this question
What if it rains or the weather is uncomfortable?

Discuss an indoor alternative, a different time or a backup date while planning. The couple, studio and venue should agree which conditions would change the plan.

For your proposal: The studio’s written policy determines rescheduling costs and availability.

Discuss this question
How early should we do the pre-wedding shoot?

Work backwards from when you need the finished material. Invitations, a wedding website or a screening need time for the shoot, editing, any agreed revisions and your own final preparation.

For your proposal: Ask the studio to confirm that your required delivery date is achievable.

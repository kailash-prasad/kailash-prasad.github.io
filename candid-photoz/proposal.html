The redesign proposal

The work deserves
a better stage.

A complete independent concept for Candid Photography, prepared by Kailash Prasad. Explore it as a visitor, then use this page to review the thinking behind it.

What this preview is

A working, responsive static website using photographs and film links from candidphotoz.com. It is hosted separately on Kailash’s website for review. The original photographer website is not changed.

Photographs remain the photographer’s work. This concept is marked noindex and is not represented as the studio’s official new website.

From unfinished template to considered experience.

Existing website

  • Unfinished gallery descriptions and blog posts.
  • Package details embedded in small poster images.
  • Photography and video mixed without clear discovery.
  • Generic captions and inconsistent category text.
  • Testimonials displayed with stock images.
  • An enquiry form without structured event dates or city.

This concept

  • A coherent editorial identity and original page copy.
  • Readable, ordered collections with explicit quotation caveats.
  • Filterable photographs, keyboard-accessible viewer and dedicated films.
  • A clear experience page and useful planning notes.
  • No invented reviews, awards, couple identities or team biographies.
  • A structured enquiry that prepares a WhatsApp message for review.

What the studio should add before an official launch

Real wedding stories

Six complete weddings with approved couple names, venues, event sequences, galleries and matching films. Current collections are deliberately not presented as one couple’s wedding.

People and provenance

Lead photographer and team portraits, biographies, genuine client reviews and evidence for the existing website’s three-generation history dating to 1942.

Confirmed service terms

Current pricing, crew per event, delivery schedules, revisions, taxes, travel, image formats and written booking terms.

A practical launch plan

  1. Approve the visual direction, tone and brand name.
  2. Confirm permissions to publish all photographs, films and client details.
  3. Supply complete wedding stories, team details and verified reviews.
  4. Confirm package inclusions and booking policies.
  5. Connect the final enquiry system and test the full response process.
  6. Move the approved website to the studio’s own domain, add canonical URLs and enable indexing.
  7. Measure qualified enquiries and bookings, then refine the pages.

The planning upgrade

A complete path
from browsing to a brief.

Built and ready to explore

Wedding and pre-wedding routes, 40 searchable answers, four sourced location guides, a complete-cost worksheet, family-photo and preparation checklists, a schedule calculator, a gallery shortlist and a structured enquiry handoff.

Kept honest

Reference package prices retain their date. Unknown gallery quantities, current terms, crew and delivery milestones are clearly identified. Complete galleries, team biographies and client reviews are not invented.

Supply before an official launch

Approve current prices, total JPEG and retouched counts, film scope, album copies, hours, team, delivery and revision milestones, payment/rescheduling terms, privacy choices and backup arrangements. Supply actual team portraits, verified reviews and permission-cleared full galleries.

Planning choices and saved looks are kept in this browser tab only after an explicit save action. Contact details are not stored. The visitor reviews the final WhatsApp message before choosing to send it.

Built to hand over

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, optimised local images, source attribution and editing instructions are included in the GitHub repository. No framework, subscription or compilation is needed for this preview.

Try your own estimates. These are planning calculations, not studio session limits, venue opening hours or a recommended shooting timetable.

Your planning allowance

A local session

Allow for arrival and preparation, a first photography block, a change and break if needed, then another block. Use actual venue access hours.

A destination route

Keep transfers separate from photography. Build the route around comfortable access, a changing base and conditions on the day.

A wedding day

Place family groups and portraits around the real ritual schedule. Share simultaneous events and avoid promising every moment from one camera.
